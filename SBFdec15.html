God's Miraculous Deliverance

Exodus 15:1-13

Context

After the final plague, and letting the Hebrews leave, Pharoah changed his mind and attempted to trap the Hebrews.

Main Idea: God is our faithful Deliverer, able and powerful enough to save us from all things.

We Worship, Knowing God is Our Strength and Deliverer

(Ex 15:1-10)

The Israelites beheld God’s glory in their deliverance from Egypt, so they sang.

This song of Moses was more than simple celebration. It was an eyewitness account of the powerful acts of God—a testimony of His mighty deeds.

There is Only One True God

(Ex 15:11-13)

The Old Testament often refers to other gods. These were real entities drawing power from the evil one. Yet with each plague and the final deliverance through the parted waters, God demonstrated that He reigns supreme. He alone is God.
. Recalling God’s past provisions enables us to trust more readily in God’s future faithfulness and His power to accomplish what He has planned.
Nowhere in this song do we hear about the courage of Moses or the swiftness of the Israelites; all the focus is on God, who deserved all the praise.
How can remembering God’s faithfulness to His people in the past help you trust in His promises yet fulfilled?
