God's Promise to Jacob

Genesis 28:10-22

Context

Despite Jacob's manipulation, Jacob was chosen by God to receive the covenant promises passed down from Abraham and Isaac.

Main Idea: God is present with His people, faithful to His promises, and deserving of our worship.

Timeline
