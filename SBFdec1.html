God's Miraculous Call

Exodus 4:1-5,10-17

Context

Through God’s sovereign and gracious provision, Jacob’s family relocated to Egypt to escape death by famine.

Main Idea: When God calls, He equips His people for service.

Timeline

  • Jacob and Family Relocate (Gen 46-50)
  • A New King Enslaves the Hebrews (Ex 1)
  • Baby Moses is Rescued (Ex 2)
  • Adult Moses Kills and Flees (Ex 2)
  • God Calls Moses (Ex 3-4)
  • God Delivers His People (Ex 5-14)

God Chooses to Partner With His People To Do His Work

(Ex 4:1-5)

We have a tendency to believe that our shortcomings and failures are somehow more significant than those of others.

What excuses do you tend to make that may be keeping you from being used by God?

How does knowing that you have been indwelled and empowered by the Holy Spirit give you courage to obey God’s calling?

God Equips Those He Calls and Provides Help Through His People

(Ex 4.10-17)

Humility is rightly seeing the sufficiency of God over any supposed sufficiency we think we have. But a wrong focus on self over God applies to our insufficiencies as well.

Where do you feel weak, and how might God work through your weaknesses?
Moses’s self-centeredness kept him from casting his gaze on God and trusting in God’s strength, sufficiency, and sovereignty. His insecurities revealed a deep-seated focus on self, which was confirmed by his outright petition for God to use someone else.
How have you seen God’s provision in your life?

Main Idea:When God calls, He equips His people for service.

How does it feel to know that God has a plan to use our weaknesses for His glory?
