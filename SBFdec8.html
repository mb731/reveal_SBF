God's Miraculous Passover

Context

Main Idea: God will execute judgment but will give grace to those who follow Him.

God Will Judge All Sinners, But He Gives Grace To Those Who Trust in Him

(Ex 12:12-13)

God is not a demanding narcissist in need of praise and honor to fill some depleted worship tank. We glorify God because it is embedded in why we exist—we were created to worship Him.

Salvation Will Come Through the Blood of the Lamb

(Ex 12:21-28)

With this final plague, God would bring Pharaoh to his knees. Yet in His mercy, God supplied a way of escape for His people.

Why do you think God called the Israelites to take an active role in their salvation by applying the blood to their door frames?
How does the Passover story lead you to worship God?
There are several signs, symbols, and pointers in the Old Testament that foreshadowed Christ as being the sacrificial Lamb of God who would take away the sins of the world. However, unlike the sacrificial system of the Old Testament, whose sacrifices were unable to take away sin (Heb. 10:4), Christ’s sacrifice on the cross was able to permanently, “once and for all,” take away sins.
